### Fixed

- DHCP agent: on a Kea HA pair, a burst of first acquisitions no longer makes both members' lease-events batches queue on the DNS zone row for the whole batch. A batch's DDNS publishes now run after its mirror rows commit, in short transactions (25 names to start, then sized from how long the last one held its zone rows, 8 to 100, so a batch makes few commits and is not slower than before), so the other member's batch waits about 2 s at most; the DNS agents are woken once for the whole POST, also when it answers 503 after some chunks committed. A connection lost in those publishes still answers 503, and the resend of the batch (a duplicate by its receipt) finishes them, publishing each name once (#3816).
