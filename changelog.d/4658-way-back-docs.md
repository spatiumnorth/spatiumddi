### Fixed

- Upgrade notes for 1.0.1: upgrading from 1.0.0 to 1.0.1 is supported. The way back is not a slot rollback. Keep the Full backup you take before the upgrade, reinstall 1.0.0 from its ISO, and restore that archive there. See "The way back from 1.0.1 to 1.0.0" in `docs/deployment/APPLIANCE.md` ([#4658](https://github.com/spatiumnorth/spatiumddi/issues/4658)).
