### Fixed

- A full (or selective, with auth/DNS/DHCP ticked) console restore onto a fresh appliance install replaced the appliance registry and the internal CA with the archive's, so the supervisor running that control plane was refused as unknown, revoked itself, tore its role pods down and cleared its node labels: DNS and DHCP stopped and no console action recovered them. The restore now keeps the appliance rows that were alive before the replay, puts this install's CA back when the archive's differs, re-points group assignments by name, and records `adopted_appliances` in the audit entry. A restore onto the same install is unchanged. The "Restoring a backup onto a fresh install" steps are in `docs/deployment/APPLIANCE.md`. (#4677)
