### Added

- `/metrics` now carries DNS, DHCP and IPAM business series: per-subnet allocated, total and utilization percent; per-DHCP-scope active leases (distinct addresses, so a Kea HA pair counts once) and enabled state; dynamic-pool occupancy; DNS and DHCP server up and config-apply state; the DNS query rate and RPZ blocks; DHCP socket drops; and open alerts by severity. Subnet and scope numbers come from the same collectors as the InfluxDB export. They are cached for 15 s, capped at 10,000 series per family, and served only on an authenticated scrape: the scrape token reads all of them, an API token only the families its owner may read through the API. They are omitted when `PROMETHEUS_METRICS_REQUIRE_AUTH=false`, and `PROMETHEUS_BUSINESS_METRICS_ENABLED=false` turns them off.
